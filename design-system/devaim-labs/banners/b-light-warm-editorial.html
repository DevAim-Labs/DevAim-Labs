Direction B — Light Warm Editorial

Art-direction style: Editorial + Illustrated (flat shapes). Mood: a well-made local magazine. It is warm, trustworthy and unhurried, and speaks to restaurant and boutique owners. Visuals: an arched "window" with a flat sun and hills illustration (pure CSS/SVG shapes in brand colours) that stands for "your business, seen", plus real project screenshots in paper-white frames once available. This is the persisted direction (see ../MASTER.md and the deltas in ../DIRECTIONS.md).

Layout: magazine masthead rule at the top (1px ink line with small caps labels), a large serif headline over 3 lines, and an arched illustration plate on the right. Margins are 96px left and right, 88px top and 64px bottom. The headline stays inside x 96–736, and the plate is decorative: if it is cropped away in a square thumbnail, no information is lost.

Type: Fraunces 400 at 76px (opsz 144, italic-free, with the key phrase set in green instead of italic for legibility at thumbnail size), and Inter 400/600 for supporting text.

Contrast: ink #1F1A14 on cream #FBF7F0 = 16.2:1; muted #5E5548 = 6.85:1; green emphasis #0F5F46 = 7.16:1. Terracotta #C2410C is used only for shapes and the CTA (white on terracotta = 5.18:1).

EN variant: "Your business deserves a website that brings in customers." / "Custom work for restaurants, shops and independents."

Composition: asymmetric 7/5 split. Left: eyebrow, serif H1, one-sentence offer, a pill CTA plus a text link, and a trust line. Right: one framed case-study card and one pull quote. That puts social proof inside the first viewport, as the landing pattern (Hero, then testimonials, then CTA) recommends.

Motion (tier 3): a single 350ms fade with 12px rise on the H1, card and quote, staggered 60ms, using power1.out. The sun shape rises 8px once. There is no parallax, and reduced motion shows the final state.

Imagery rule: replace the CSS illustration with real client screenshots as soon as they exist. Never use stock photos of laptops. Every testimonial must be real, with name and role; the quote shown here is a placeholder.

Mobile: single column; the card moves below the CTA and the quote below the card. The H1 is 40px.
