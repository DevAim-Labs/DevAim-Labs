Composition: a scrolling service ticker (it pauses on hover and focus, and is static under reduced motion), a massive H1 with a highlighter word, and a lead form in the hero with 2 visible, labelled fields (UX guidance: labels, not placeholders; loading, then success state on submit). The stat row replaces logos until real client logos exist. Use only verifiable claims.

Motion (tier 6): the highlighter wipes in left to right over 400ms after the H1 appears; the sticker and form drop in with back.out(1.4) at 300–450ms; buttons press into their shadow (translate 4px, shadow 0) at 120ms. Reduced motion shows the final state.

Mobile: H1 at 52px, and the form moves directly under the CTA. The primary CTA becomes a sticky bottom bar after the hero scrolls out.
