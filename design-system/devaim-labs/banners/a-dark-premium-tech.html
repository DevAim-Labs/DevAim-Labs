Composition: 12-column grid, copy on columns 1–6, a stack of two floating UI cards on columns 8–12, offset by 24px and slightly overlapping for depth (no 3D, no perspective tilt, so it stays sharp and cheap to render).

Motion (tier 6): on load the cards fade and rise 16px, staggered 80ms; the KPI number counts up once (600ms, ease-out); the glow drifts ±20px over 12s. Under prefers-reduced-motion everything renders in its final state and the glow stays still.

Mobile (<768px): cards stack below the CTA at 90% width, and the glow shrinks to 360px. The hero must never exceed 100svh before the CTA is visible.

Assets: none external. The cards are real HTML, so the numbers can be swapped per page (restaurant: reservations; store: orders; dashboards: KPIs).
