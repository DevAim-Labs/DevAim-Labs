# Three directions for the new DevAim Labs site

Each direction below comes from the competitor research (10 sites), the design tokens extracted from 5 benchmark sites and the UI/UX design database. The previews use your own copy, colours and fonts. Pick one and I'll build it at `/v2`.

Gap in the market**No Dutch competitor pairs a modern design with public prices.**4 of 6 Dutch studios hide pricing, and none shows live demos or dashboards. You have both.

What converts**Outcome headline, proof before the second call to action, "vanaf" prices.**Seen on In Your Face Media, Studio Brabo and Designjoy.

Lead magnet**A free website check, and a reply within one working day.**Short forms (2–4 fields) with visible labels. Your face near the button.

Avoid**All-dark pages, flat type, heavy scroll effects.**These put off restaurant and shop owners and slow down mid-range phones.

strongest for dashboards & integrations

Near-black with one blue accent and mono numbers, like Linear and basement.studio. Your dashboards, payments and API work look high-end. Restaurant and shop owners may find it cold.

Cream paper, a serif headline, forest green and a terracotta call-to-action. It feels local, crafted and trustworthy, which suits restaurants and boutiques. It has the best contrast and the lightest weight on phones.

### Palette

Background`#FBF7F0`

Ink`#1F1A14`

Forest (primary)`#0F5F46`

Sand sections`#F1E7D6`

### Type & motion

- Fraunces for headings, Inter for body text
- 12px cards, pill buttons, arched image plates, warm soft shadows
- Calm: one 350ms fade-and-rise on scroll, no parallax, no loops

#### Good

- Closest fit to your main buyers
- Flatters client screenshots
- Suits the formal "u" tone

#### Watch out

- Weaker signal for dashboards (fix with dark demo panels)
- Cream + serif + terracotta is a common look; needs your real work to feel yours
