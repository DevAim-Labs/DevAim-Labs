Three directions for the new DevAim Labs site

Each direction below comes from the competitor research (10 sites), the design tokens extracted from 5 benchmark sites and the UI/UX design database. The previews use your own copy, colours and fonts. Pick one and I'll build it at /v2.

Gap in the marketNo Dutch competitor pairs a modern design with public prices.4 of 6 Dutch studios hide pricing, and none shows live demos or dashboards. You have both.
What convertsOutcome headline, proof before the second call to action, "vanaf" prices.Seen on In Your Face Media, Studio Brabo and Designjoy.
Lead magnetA free website check, and a reply within one working day.Short forms (2–4 fields) with visible labels. Your face near the button.
AvoidAll-dark pages, flat type, heavy scroll effects.These put off restaurant and shop owners and slow down mid-range phones.

ADark premium tech

strongest for dashboards & integrations

Near-black with one blue accent and mono numbers, like Linear and basement.studio. Your dashboards, payments and API work look high-end. Restaurant and shop owners may find it cold.

Button labels must be dark; white text on this blue fails contrast (3.2:1).

Type & motion

  • Geist for headings and UI, JetBrains Mono for numbers
  • Cards 16px radius, 1px borders, soft blue glow
  • Staggered reveals, KPI count-ups, slow glow drift (300–450ms)

Good

  • Signals technical skill
  • Dashboards look great
  • Stands out from template agencies

Watch out

  • Can feel cold to shop owners
  • Light client sites clash with a dark frame
  • Glow costs performance

BLight warm editorial

recommended base

Cream paper, a serif headline, forest green and a terracotta call-to-action. It feels local, crafted and trustworthy, which suits restaurants and boutiques. It has the best contrast and the lightest weight on phones.

Uw zaak verdient een website die klanten oplevert.

Palette

Background#FBF7F0
Ink#1F1A14
Forest (primary)#0F5F46
Terracotta (actions only)#C2410C
Sand sections#F1E7D6

All text pairs pass AA. White on terracotta is 5.2:1.

Type & motion

  • Fraunces for headings, Inter for body text
  • 12px cards, pill buttons, arched image plates, warm soft shadows
  • Calm: one 350ms fade-and-rise on scroll, no parallax, no loops

Good

  • Closest fit to your main buyers
  • Flatters client screenshots
  • Suits the formal "u" tone

Watch out

  • Weaker signal for dashboards (fix with dark demo panels)
  • Cream + serif + terracotta is a common look; needs your real work to feel yours

CBold conversion agency

highest expected lead rate

Big heavy type, a lime highlighter, hard borders and offset shadows. The form sits in the hero. It's the most memorable and works well for ads, but it can feel salesy to premium boutiques.

  • Less suited to refined client designs

My recommendation: B, with two borrowed pieces

Use B for the whole sales page, because it fits restaurant and shop owners and has the lowest accessibility and performance risk. To avoid the generic cream-and-serif look and cover your software buyers too, add:

  1. From A: dark panels for the live demos and the dashboard, payments and API cases, with mono numbers and real UI cards. This is the part no Dutch competitor has.
  2. From C: the two-field website check form in the hero, the stat row and a sticky call-to-action on mobile.

Reply in the terminal with A, B, C or B + mix, plus any changes to the colours or fonts.
