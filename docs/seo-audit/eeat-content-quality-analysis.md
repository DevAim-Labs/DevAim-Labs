## Executive Summary

**Overall Content Quality Score: 62/100**

DevAim Labs presents a clean, well-structured website with good technical implementation but significant E-E-A-T gaps. The site demonstrates basic trustworthiness through business registration details but lacks the depth of experience signals, expertise demonstration, and authoritative content expected for a professional software development agency.

**Critical Issues:**
- No team member information or credentials
- Missing case studies with measurable outcomes
- Thin content on most pages (below recommended minimums)
- No client testimonials or social proof
- Limited technical depth in service descriptions
- No blog or thought leadership content
- Minimal first-hand experience signals

## AI Citation Readiness: 35/100

### What AI Systems Look For
Modern AI systems (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google SGE) prefer content with:
- Clear, quotable facts
- Statistics and measurable data
- Structured lists
- Direct answers to common questions
- Attribution and sources

### Current Assessment

**Strengths:**
- FAQ schema markup (well-structured Q&A)
- Clear service categories
- Specific technology names (citeable)
- Business registration numbers (verifiable facts)

**Weaknesses:**
- No statistics or data points to quote
- No "According to DevAim Labs..." quotable statements
- Missing industry insights or research
- No comparison tables (e.g., "Laravel vs WordPress for dashboards")
- No definitive guides or resources
- Lack of original perspective/unique methodology

**Example of Non-Quotable Content:**
> "Wij zijn het team dat jouw adminpaneel, dashboard of integratie bouwt."

**How to Make Quotable:**
> "DevAim Labs ontwikkelt adminpanelen en KPI-dashboards die gemiddeld 15 uur per week aan handmatige data-invoer besparen voor Nederlandse MKB-bedrijven. Onze Laravel-Vue stack zorgt voor real-time dashboards met responstijden onder 200ms."

**Recommendations:**
1. Add statistics from own projects (anonymized if needed)
2. Create comparison guides:
   - "5 signalen dat je Excel moet vervangen door een dashboard"
   - "Mollie vs Stripe: welke past bij Nederlandse webshops?"
3. Build quotable resources:
   - "De 7-stappen checklist voor maatwerksoftware"
   - "Gemiddelde kosten adminpaneel Nederland 2026"
4. Add FAQ answers with specific data
5. Create glossary of Dutch software terms

## Conclusion

DevAim Labs has a solid technical foundation but needs significant content expansion and E-E-A-T enhancement to compete effectively in organic search and build trust with potential clients.

**Key Strengths:**
- Good technical implementation (structured data, meta tags)
- Clear service positioning
- Trustworthy business registration details
- User-friendly Dutch language

**Critical Gaps:**
- Severely thin content across all pages
- Missing team and expertise demonstration
- No portfolio/case studies
- Lack of authoritative content (blog, guides, thought leadership)
- Minimal first-hand experience signals

**Impact on Search Visibility:**
Current site likely struggles with:
- Ranking for competitive terms ("maatwerksoftware", "adminpaneel laten maken")
- Appearing in AI-generated answers
- Converting cold traffic (lack of trust signals)

**Estimated Time to Reach Strong E-E-A-T (75+ score):**
- With dedicated effort: 6-9 months
- With moderate effort: 12-18 months

**Return on Investment:**
Strong E-E-A-T content typically shows:
